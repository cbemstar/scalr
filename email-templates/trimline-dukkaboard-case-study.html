Q. How do you keep a high-volume hotel build on schedule – without compromising quality?
A. Create a bespoke tray and drain solution that saves significant construction time per bathroom.
In the world of hotel construction, when build programmes are tight and bathroom numbers are high, every day counts. Both in terms of cost and overall completion time. And every inconsistency on site causes further expensive delays.
So when specifiers at luxury wellness hotel Six Senses at The Whiteley needed a bathroom tray and drain solution that would meet the demands of an ambitious build schedule, without sacrificing quality or technical consistency, they looked to Trimline.
Our Dukkaboard team duly stepped up to deliver exactly that – and exceeded all expectations in the process.
Here’s how it worked:
Bespoke to every bathroom
Each tray was manufactured to the precise dimensions of its bathroom, with an impressive 7-10 day lead time from site measure to delivery.
Engineered for direct site installation
The tray was designed to install directly onto the concrete slab – completely removing the need to build a plywood kerb before fitting tile backer board.
Minimal build-up, full compliance
Our solution offered a 59mm build-up and full BBA compliance, with everything engineered to meet real site constraints without compromising performance.
Start-to-finish technical support
Dukkaboard’s technical team was on-site throughout, supporting installation and bathroom set-out to help ensure consistent quality across every unit.
Significant time and cost savings
Our bespoke solution delivered on all levels for the client, with an impressive 7 days of construction time saved per bathroom.
This is what a solutions-led approach looks like:
quality products engineered for the realities of your project, backed by the technical expertise to ensure accurate specification and installation every time.
